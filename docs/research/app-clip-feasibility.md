# App Clip feasibility: Persona onboarding and product discovery from an iMessage thread

Research pass, 2026-09-26. Apple's developer pages are JS-rendered, so they were read through the
Markdown/JSON endpoints behind `developer.apple.com`; each claim is followed by the page it was verified
on. `developer.apple.com/documentation/app_clips` and `developer.apple.com/app-clips/` both resolve to
`developer.apple.com/documentation/appclip`, which is what is cited. UNVERIFIED marks anything no primary
Apple source confirmed. Sibling doc: `app-clips-in-messages.md` (Gmail-connect angle); this brief covers
the "show what Persona can do / wristband / other products / full-experience preview" angle.

## Verdict

- (a) Web-simulator demo of an App Clip card inside the mock thread: feasible today with zero Apple dependencies. Build the card to the spec in section 3 (1800x1200 3:2 image, title ≤30 chars, subtitle ≤56 chars, verb View/Play/Open, App Store link, optional notifications note).
- (b) Real App Clip card in iMessage on a device: feasible only after Persona has a native iOS app with an App Clip target, a paid Apple Developer Program membership, and, for anyone other than your own test devices, an App Store-approved and released version. A Local Experience can show the card on your own iPhone before review, but still needs the paid program to sign the clip.
- Top blockers: no iOS app exists; the clip must be native Swift/SwiftUI, not a web view; the sender must be in the recipient's Contacts; the HIG and Review Guidelines reject marketing/advertising-only clips, so "products and how to get them" must be reframed as a usable demo; card content is static per App Store Connect experience.

## 1. What an App Clip is, technically

- "An App Clip is a lightweight version of your app that offers access to some of the app's functionality"; it is not on the Home Screen and "the system removes an App Clip from a device after a period of inactivity" (https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appclip). Availability is iOS/iPadOS 14+ (https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appclip; https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/offer-app-clip-experiences/overview-of-app-clips).
- It is a target inside the full app's Xcode project: "App Clips require a corresponding full app that offers at least the same functionality as the App Clip; you use the same Xcode project for your full app and your App Clip", and "Xcode creates a new build phase for the app target that embeds the App Clip in the app" (https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appclip/creating-an-app-clip-with-xcode).
- Native code: "App Clips make use of SwiftUI and UIKit, and have access to the same frameworks as your full app" (https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appclip/choosing-the-right-functionality-for-your-app-clip). It supports "the same devices as the full app, not including macOS" (https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appclip/creating-an-app-clip-with-xcode).
- Identity: the app identifier is the full app's identifier plus a suffix, e.g. `$(AppIdentifierPrefix)com.example.MyApp.Clip`; Xcode adds the On Demand Install Capable entitlement and the Parent Application Identifiers entitlement (https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appclip/creating-an-app-clip-with-xcode); that entitlement must hold "exactly one entry, the corresponding app's application identifier" (https://developer.apple.com/documentation/bundleresources/entitlements/com.apple.developer.parent-application-identifiers).
- No parent app, no clip: "An App Clip always requires a corresponding full app, and you submit your App Clip binary together with your full app's binary to App Store Connect" (https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appclip/configuring-the-launch-experience-of-your-app-clip); "you don't submit the App Clip on its own; you export and submit the app bundle of the full app that includes the App Clip", and "Enterprise distribution isn't available to App Clips" (https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appclip/distributing-your-app-clip); "To edit the App Clip section in App Store Connect, you must first upload a build that includes an App Clip" (https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/offer-app-clip-experiences/overview-of-app-clips).
- Must be approved and released before public invocations work: "Testing and using default and demo links requires your app and App Clip to pass App Store review and to be available in the App Store" (https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appclip/configuring-the-launch-experience-of-your-app-clip); the Smart App Banner appears only if "You published a version of your app that offers an App Clip" (https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appclip/supporting-invocations-from-your-website-and-the-messages-app); the generated default link and demo URL "will become active once your version has been approved" (https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/offer-app-clip-experiences/offer-a-default-app-clip-experience). Pre-approval exceptions are Local Experiences and TestFlight (section 6).
- TestFlight: "If your app includes an App Clip, you can invite testers to test the App Clip experience on its own"; "Either the app or the App Clip can be installed on a device at a time" (https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/test-a-beta-version/testflight-overview). Up to three invocation URLs per build; "Currently, testing of other invocation methods is not supported" (https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/test-a-beta-version/test-an-app-clip-experience). "The App Clip card doesn't appear for App Clip experiences you create in TestFlight", and TestFlight experiences require associating the clip with your website (https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appclip/testing-the-launch-experience-of-your-app-clip).

## 2. Size limits (uncompressed App Clip binary, per thinned variant)

- Apple's current table: iOS 15 and earlier 10 MB; iOS 16 15 MB; iOS 17 and later 100 MB "with additional requirements and limitations" (https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appclip/choosing-the-right-functionality-for-your-app-clip). App Store Connect's reference matches: "iOS 17 and later 100 MB", "iOS 16 and later 15 MB", "Earlier than iOS 16 10 MB", measured on "the uncompressed main bundle of any App Clip variant, after app thinning" (https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/maximum-build-file-sizes).
- The 100 MB tier applies only if the clip "only supports digital invocations — for example, from your website or Spotlight search", "doesn't support physical invocations such as App Clip Codes, QR codes, or NFC tags", is used "where a reliable internet connection is likely", and "doesn't support iOS 16 and earlier"; the App Store Connect demo link lets a 100 MB clip also support physical invocations (https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appclip/choosing-the-right-functionality-for-your-app-clip; https://developer.apple.com/documentation/updates/appclips).
- The 50 MB figure is historical: WWDC23 announced "a new 50 MB size limit for digital invocations" in iOS 17, "the 15 MB limitation introduced in iOS 16" for physical invocations, and "If you're targeting iOS 15 or earlier, the original 10 MB limitation still applies" (https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2023/10178/). The docs now say 100 MB under the same digital-only conditions; the date of the 50 to 100 MB change is UNVERIFIED (the June 2025 changelog only mentions the demo URL at 100 MB, https://developer.apple.com/documentation/updates/appclips).
- Measure with an Ad Hoc/Development export with App Thinning and read `App Thinning Size Report.txt` (https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appclip/creating-an-app-clip-with-xcode). A Messages-launched Persona clip is a digital invocation: 100 MB if it is iOS 17+ only, 15 MB if iOS 16 must be supported.

## 3. Invocation from Messages

- Messages is a listed invocation: "Tapping a link that someone shares in the Messages app (as a text message only)" (https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appclip/configuring-the-launch-experience-of-your-app-clip).
- Two link types work: a link to your website carrying the Smart App Banner meta tag ("the recipient can tap the link to instantly launch your App Clip"), or Apple's default App Clip URL / demo URL ("If people share the default App Clip URL or the App Clip demo URL in Messages, Messages displays the App Clip card") (https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appclip/supporting-invocations-from-your-website-and-the-messages-app). Support matrix: "A shared link to an App Clip in the Messages app" is Yes for the default link, an associated website, and advanced experiences, and "Yes, with a limited preview" for the demo link (https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appclip/configuring-the-launch-experience-of-your-app-clip).
- Contacts requirement, verified: "Sharing your App Clip in Messages requires that the recipient's device: Runs iOS 14 or later; Contains the sender as a contact in the Contacts app" (https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appclip/supporting-invocations-from-your-website-and-the-messages-app). Apple's own test steps repeat it: "Make sure to add the sender of the message as a contact on the test device" (https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appclip/testing-the-launch-experience-of-your-app-clip).
- SMS: "If a person shares the link with someone else as an SMS, the recipient must opt to load the rich link before they can tap the preview to launch the App Clip" (https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appclip/supporting-invocations-from-your-website-and-the-messages-app).
- What the card shows and where it comes from: the default App Clip experience in App Store Connect takes "a header image, subtitle, and action. This metadata appears on the App Clip card when the experience is invoked from Safari or Messages"; the image must be .jpg/.jpeg/.png, 3:2, 1800 x 1200 px (https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/offer-app-clip-experiences/offer-a-default-app-clip-experience). WWDC20: "This metadata will be used in a Smart App Banner in Safari, and in the app clip link bubble in Messages. And also in the app clip card" (https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2020/10146/). Title up to 30 characters, subtitle up to 56 (https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appclip/configuring-the-launch-experience-of-your-app-clip). Verbs: "Possible verbs are View, Play, or Open. Pick View for media, or if your App Clip provides informational or educational content. Pick Play for games. Choose Open for all other App Clips"; image "1800x1200 px PNG or JPEG image without transparency", no UI screenshots, no text in the image (https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/app-clips). The card also links to the store: "On the App Clip card, people can either launch the App Clip or visit the full app's page on the App Store" (https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/app-clips). With ephemeral notifications enabled, the card carries a note the user can tap to disable them (https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appclip/enabling-notifications-in-app-clips).
- Static: "it's not possible to programmatically change the content on the App Clip card"; each context that needs its own card needs its own advanced experience (https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appclip/configuring-the-launch-experience-of-your-app-clip). The Messages preview image comes from `og:image` on each page (https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appclip/supporting-invocations-from-your-website-and-the-messages-app).
- URL the clip receives: "Invocations from Messages app or your website use the site's URL as the invocation URL" (https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appclip/responding-to-invocations).
- When the clip is unavailable: the `app-id` in the meta tag lets "the Smart App Banner open the full app on devices that run iOS 13 or earlier and on devices where Screen Time or a mobile device management (MDM) profile don't allow App Clips" (https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appclip/supporting-invocations-from-your-website-and-the-messages-app); "App Clips aren't available to children under 13 or those with Ask to Buy enabled" (https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/offer-app-clip-experiences/overview-of-app-clips); a Screen Time "Don't Allow" deletes existing clips (https://support.apple.com/en-us/102093). Apple's only stated Messages fallback: "When a person taps a link to a website that displays the Smart App Banner in the Messages app, the invocation URL of your App Clip opens in the default browser" (https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appclip/supporting-invocations-from-your-website-and-the-messages-app). What renders when the sender is not in Contacts or the app is unpublished is not spelled out; expect an ordinary link preview (UNVERIFIED).
- Non-Apple report with no Apple reply: a developer saw the Messages card pass the URL without its query string when the Open button rather than the image was tapped (https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/745175) (UNVERIFIED).

## 4. Website requirements

- File: `apple-app-site-association` (no extension) at `https://<fully qualified domain>/.well-known/apple-app-site-association`; "You must host the file using https:// with a valid certificate and with no redirects"; every subdomain needs its own file and its own entitlement entry (https://developer.apple.com/documentation/xcode/supporting-associated-domains). No Content-Type requirement is stated on that page (UNVERIFIED).
- Key format: `{"appclips": {"apps": ["ABCDE12345.com.example.MyApp.Clip"]}}`, i.e. `<Application Identifier Prefix>.<Bundle Identifier>` (https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appclip/associating-your-app-clip-with-your-website; https://developer.apple.com/documentation/xcode/supporting-associated-domains). The server must allow the `AASA-Bot` and `CFNetwork` user agents (https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appclip/associating-your-app-clip-with-your-website).
- Entitlement: add Associated Domains to both the app and the App Clip targets with `appclips:<fully qualified domain>` (e.g. `appclips:example.com`), with no trailing slash, wildcard, path, or query (https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appclip/associating-your-app-clip-with-your-website); services are `webcredentials`, `applinks`, `activitycontinuation`, `appclips` (https://developer.apple.com/documentation/bundleresources/entitlements/com.apple.developer.associated-domains); a `*.` prefix is allowed only "for services other than appclips" (https://developer.apple.com/documentation/xcode/supporting-associated-domains).
- Delivery: since iOS 14 devices fetch the AASA via Apple's CDN, which requests it within 24 hours; devices re-check roughly weekly; `?mode=developer` bypasses the CDN for Developer Mode devices and development-signed builds only (https://developer.apple.com/documentation/bundleresources/entitlements/com.apple.developer.associated-domains; https://developer.apple.com/documentation/xcode/supporting-associated-domains).
- Meta tag: `<meta name="apple-itunes-app" content="app-id=myAppStoreID, app-clip-bundle-id=appClipBundleID, app-clip-display=card">`; `app-clip-display=card` shows the card in Safari on iOS 15+; the banner needs the association, the tag, a configured default experience, a published version, and Safari without Private Browsing; `app-argument` is not passed to clips (https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appclip/supporting-invocations-from-your-website-and-the-messages-app).
- Association is optional if you use only the Apple-generated default link `https://appclip.apple.com/id?p=<app clip bundle id>&key=value` (iOS 16.4+), which does not support the website banner/card, App Clip Codes, or Maps (https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appclip/configuring-the-launch-experience-of-your-app-clip; https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/offer-app-clip-experiences/offer-a-default-app-clip-experience). App Store Connect shows per-domain validation plus a Load Debug Status check (https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appclip/associating-your-app-clip-with-your-website).

## 5. Capabilities and limits

- Frameworks that "provide no functionality at runtime": AppIntents, AssetsLibrary, BackgroundTasks, CallKit, CareKit, Contacts, ContactsUI, CoreMotion, EventKit, EventKitUI, FileProvider, FileProviderUI, HealthKit, HomeKit, MediaPlayer, Messages, MessageUI, NearbyInteraction, PhotoKit, ResearchKit, SensorKit, Speech (https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appclip/choosing-the-right-functionality-for-your-app-clip).
- No background execution: no background URLSession, no Background Modes, no Bluetooth connections while not in use; no app extensions except a widget extension for Live Activities; no Face ID (Touch ID via LocalAuthentication works); CloudKit is read-only public database from iOS 16; `ASWebAuthenticationSession` is allowed for auth providers (https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appclip/choosing-the-right-functionality-for-your-app-clip).
- Privacy: no SKAdNetwork, no App Tracking Transparency, `identifierForVendor` is empty, location only When In Use (resets at 4:00 a.m. the next day), no access to Apple Music/Media, to Calendar/Contacts/Files/Health/Messages/Reminders/Photos data, or to motion/fitness data; "App Clips can't share data with any other app except its corresponding full app" (https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appclip/choosing-the-right-functionality-for-your-app-clip).
- Notifications: with `NSAppClipRequestEphemeralUserNotification` set to true the clip can "schedule or receive notifications for up to 8 hours after each launch"; anything longer needs an explicit permission request (https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appclip/enabling-notifications-in-app-clips; https://developer.apple.com/documentation/bundleresources/information-property-list/nsappclip/nsappcliprequestephemeralusernotification). HIG: "Push notifications aren't a good way to ask people to install the app" (https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/app-clips).
- Payments and identity: the HIG recommends Apple Pay and Sign in with Apple (https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/app-clips); "App Clips may use any technology to allow users to sign in", with Sign in with Apple recommended (https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appclip/sharing-data-between-your-app-clip-and-your-full-app); "Sign in with Apple must be enabled on the parent iOS App ID if you would like to use this feature on associated App Clips" (https://developer.apple.com/help/account/identifiers/enable-app-capabilities/). In-App Purchase is listed among functionality to "reserve for the full app" for in-the-moment clips, alongside promoting other apps, custom URL schemes, settings, and review requests (https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appclip/choosing-the-right-functionality-for-your-app-clip); whether StoreKit purchases are technically blocked inside a clip is UNVERIFIED.
- Persistence: "App clips are automatically removed from your device after 30 days of non-use"; "Data associated with an App Clip is deleted from your device after 10 days or non-use, or, if you have signed in to the App Clip with Sign in with Apple, after 30 days of non-use" (https://support.apple.com/en-us/102093). Developer docs say only "a period of inactivity" (https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appclip); WWDC20: "treat it more like a temporary cache" and "App Clips are not included in iOS backups" (https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2020/10174/).
- Sharing with the full app: App Groups shared container and `UserDefaults(suiteName:)`; keychain items the clip writes are readable by the full app from iOS 15.4, but the clip can never read the app's keychain; "an App Clip can only share its data with its corresponding app" (https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appclip/sharing-data-between-your-app-clip-and-your-full-app); "App Groups for App Clips may only share data between the parent iOS app and the associated App Clip" (https://developer.apple.com/help/account/identifiers/enable-app-capabilities/). Upsell via `SKOverlay.AppClipConfiguration`; "Don't require users to install your app to complete a task" (https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appclip/recommending-your-app-to-app-clip-users).
- Web view versus native: the HIG says "Avoid using web views in your App Clip. App Clips use native components and frameworks to offer an app-quality experience. If only web components are available to you, offer a quick link to your website instead of an App Clip" (https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/app-clips). The App Store Review Guidelines have no clip-specific native/web rule; the only App Clip text is 2.5.16(a): "all App Clip features and functionality must be included in the main app binary. App Clips cannot contain advertising", and 2.5.18: display advertising "should not be included in extensions, App Clips, widgets, notifications, keyboards, watchOS apps, etc." (https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/). General rules that still apply: 4.2 "Your app should include features, content, and UI that elevate it beyond a repackaged website" and 4.2.2 "apps shouldn't primarily be marketing materials, advertisements, web clippings, content aggregators, or a collection of links" (https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/).
- Marketing content: "Don't use App Clips solely for marketing purposes. App Clips need to provide real value and help people accomplish tasks. Don't use them as a means to advertise services or products, and don't display ads in your App Clip"; demo clips are endorsed: "Consider creating an App Clip to let people experience your app or game before committing to a purchase or subscription" and "Don't require people to install the full app to experience the entire demo" (https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/app-clips). Multiple experiences must share one App Clip target, and "the full app must include the same functionality as the App Clip" (https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appclip/choosing-the-right-functionality-for-your-app-clip).

## 6. Review and account requirements

- Paid membership: the Apple Developer Program is a "$99 annual membership" and is what lets you "Invite testers and manage beta builds with TestFlight" and "Distribute your apps" (https://developer.apple.com/programs/). A free Apple Account gets "On-device testing using Xcode" but not Certificates, Identifiers & Profiles, App Store Connect, or TestFlight (https://developer.apple.com/support/compare-memberships/).
- Capabilities by membership: "On Demand Install Capable" (the entitlement Xcode adds to every App Clip target) is available only to Apple Developer Program members, not to the Enterprise program and not to free accounts; "Associated domains" and "Push notifications" need a paid program; "Sign in with Apple", "Apple Pay", and "In-App Purchase" are Program-only (https://developer.apple.com/help/account/reference/supported-capabilities-ios/). Inference from that table: an App Clip target cannot be provisioned for a device with a free Personal Team.
- Review: the clip is reviewed as part of the app version; you may give App Review "up to three URLs that are representative of experiences your App Clip provides"; "Ensure that each subsequent app version you submit includes the App Clip to maintain its availability for users" (https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/offer-app-clip-experiences/overview-of-app-clips).
- Default versus advanced: "A default App Clip experience is required and can be invoked using Safari and Messages, as well as through an NFC tag, QR code, and links in other apps when using an Apple-provided App Clip link"; advanced experiences add multiple domains, physical locations (Maps, via place association and Apple Business Connect), and per-business cards, and are submitted with "Send to Apple" (https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/offer-app-clip-experiences/overview-of-app-clips; https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/offer-app-clip-experiences/offer-an-advanced-app-clip-experience/). Advanced experiences are needed for full invocation support on iOS 16.3 and earlier, extra domains, locations, or multi-business clips (https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appclip/configuring-the-launch-experience-of-your-app-clip).
- Local Experiences (no review): build and run the clip on the device, then Settings > Developer > Local Experiences > Register Local Experience with a URL prefix, the App Clip bundle ID, title, subtitle, call-to-action, and header photo; launch it by scanning an App Clip Code/QR/NFC, tapping a Smart App Banner or card in Safari, or "Sharing a link to the website that displays a Smart App Banner. Make sure to add the sender of the message as a contact on the test device"; no associated domains are needed for codes/QR/NFC; local experiences "only launch an App Clip that's signed for Development, Ad Hoc, or TestFlight distribution" and "take precedence over App Clip experiences you configure in App Store Connect" (https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appclip/testing-the-launch-experience-of-your-app-clip). The same page ties Safari banner/card testing to an approved, released version, so the Messages route for a local experience with an unpublished app is documented but not demonstrated; verify on device.
- Xcode debugging: the `_XCAppClipURL` scheme variable injects an invocation URL, but "the App Clip card doesn't appear"; Settings > Developer also offers Clear Experience Cache and App Clips Diagnostics for released clips (https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appclip/testing-the-launch-experience-of-your-app-clip). Running from Xcode needs Developer Mode on the device (https://developer.apple.com/documentation/xcode/enabling-developer-mode-on-a-device).

## 7. Effort and blockers for a web-only team

Minimum path to a real App Clip card in Messages on a device. Steps 1 to 7 give the card on your own test iPhone before review; 8 to 11 make it public.

1. Enroll in the Apple Developer Program, $99/year (https://developer.apple.com/programs/).
2. Create an iOS app in Xcode and add an App Clip target (SwiftUI); the clip may only offer a subset of what the full app offers (https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appclip/creating-an-app-clip-with-xcode).
3. Build the clip's screens natively; keep the thinned binary at or under 15 MB (iOS 16+) or 100 MB (iOS 17+ digital-only) (https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/maximum-build-file-sizes).
4. Serve a page on the Persona domain with the `apple-itunes-app` meta tag (`app-clip-bundle-id`, `app-clip-display=card`) and `og:image` (https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appclip/supporting-invocations-from-your-website-and-the-messages-app).
5. Enable Developer Mode, run the clip on the test iPhone from Xcode, and register a Local Experience for that URL with the card metadata (https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appclip/testing-the-launch-experience-of-your-app-clip).
6. Add the sending number or Apple ID to the test iPhone's Contacts and send the URL as a plain iMessage text (https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appclip/supporting-invocations-from-your-website-and-the-messages-app).
7. Tap the bubble; the card and clip launch because local experiences launch dev-signed clips (https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appclip/testing-the-launch-experience-of-your-app-clip).
8. Host the AASA with the `appclips` key at `/.well-known/` and add `appclips:` associated domains to both targets (https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appclip/associating-your-app-clip-with-your-website).
9. Archive the full app, upload to App Store Connect, and configure the default App Clip experience (1800x1200 image, subtitle, verb) (https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/offer-app-clip-experiences/offer-a-default-app-clip-experience).
10. Pass App Review and release; then share the site URL or the `appclip.apple.com` default link in iMessage (https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appclip/configuring-the-launch-experience-of-your-app-clip).
11. Optionally run TestFlight in between, knowing the card does not render there (https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appclip/testing-the-launch-experience-of-your-app-clip).

Without any Apple account you can: build the web simulator's card to spec; write the SwiftUI clip and run it in Simulator (Apple says to "run the App Clip in Simulator or on a device" before adding code; that Simulator needs no provisioning is general Xcode behaviour, UNVERIFIED for clip targets) (https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appclip/creating-an-app-clip-with-xcode); prepare the AASA, meta tags, and `og:image` on the site; prepare the 1800x1200 card art, a title of at most 30 characters, and a subtitle of at most 56. Anything on a physical device needs the paid program (section 6).

Effort estimate (mine, not Apple's): enrollment one to two days for an individual, up to weeks for an organization that needs a D-U-N-S number; a first shippable iOS app plus clip for a Swift-capable engineer two to four weeks; review cycles are usually days, but every rejection restarts the clock.

Blockers, ranked:

1. No native iOS app exists, and a clip cannot ship without an approved parent that contains all clip functionality (2.5.16(a), https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/; https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appclip/configuring-the-launch-experience-of-your-app-clip).
2. The clip must be native; a WebView wrapper of the current web app is against the HIG and guideline 4.2 (https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/app-clips; https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/).
3. Content framing: "what Persona can do, the wristband, other products, how to get them" reads as marketing or a catalog, which the HIG and 2.5.16(a) reject; reframe as a demo clip in which the user actually talks to Persona, with product information incidental, and a "full experience" upsell via SKOverlay after the demo (https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/app-clips; https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appclip/recommending-your-app-to-app-clip-users).
4. The sender must be in the recipient's Contacts and the message must be an iMessage text; first-contact outreach from Persona will not get a card (https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appclip/supporting-invocations-from-your-website-and-the-messages-app).
5. Card copy and image are fixed per App Store Connect experience; no per-user personalization (https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appclip/configuring-the-launch-experience-of-your-app-clip).
6. Nothing renders publicly until App Review approves and the version is released; TestFlight shows no card (https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appclip/testing-the-launch-experience-of-your-app-clip).
7. Clip data is disposable (10/30-day deletion, no backups), and clips get no Contacts/Calendar/Health data, no background work, and no CallKit (https://support.apple.com/en-us/102093; https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appclip/choosing-the-right-functionality-for-your-app-clip).
